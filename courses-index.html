PROGRAM DETAILS, FINANCIAL INFORMATION, & ENROLLMENT REQUIREMENTS

ENROLLMENT REQUIREMENTS

- Applicants must be at least 15 years or older.

- Provide proof of valid state or federal issued photo ID.

- Must be able to commit to our in-person schedule (no exceptions)

FINANCIAL INFORMATION

- Tuition: $10,000

- Tax: $860

- Application Fee (non-refundable): $500

- Student Kit (optional, non-refundable): $1,500 (price may vary based on product/material costs)

Total Cost with Kit: $12,860

Total Cost without Kit: $11,360
-Payment Plans: Available with a minimum of $500 deposit. Remaining balance may be divided into monthly payments starting at 3% interest over 12-24 month contract.

Discounted Specials:
Occasionally offered. Amounts vary by session - please contact admissions for current offers.

PROGRAM DETAILS

Barber Program Hours:

- 1,000 total hours (mandatory in-person training at the academy with hands-on instruction)

Final 100 Hours

- Students may complete their final 100 hours through externship at a barbershop of their choice hours will be credited toward the total.

In Person Schedule

- Minimum of 3 days per week

Full-Time & Part-time available

- Classes available in English & Espanol

Schedule (No Exceptions): Monday - Friday | 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM
